Standalone experimental wrapper (eight variants, two cohorts, chromosome 22):
& $py src/select_wrapper.py --sumstats path/to/pop1.ma path/to/pop2.ma --ld path/to/pop1_ld path/to/pop2_ld --lambda 2.5 --frequency-cutoff 0.9 --bootstraps 100 --seed 123 --out work/example_output
The example lambda and cutoff are syntax examples, NOT recommended GWAS thresholds.
Use independently calibrated values for the applicable data-generating setting.
Input .ma fields: SNP A1 A2 freq b se p N. Signed .ld has SNP1 SNP2 R, no header,
including all upper-triangle entries and diagonals. Each LD prefix needs .bim and
.frq. N must be constant within a cohort in this validated research release.
The wrapper harmonizes alleles, bootstraps the summary model in R, and calls the
unchanged official executable in --cojo-joint mode. It never edits that executable.
